Our Story

Born from a love for good food and the open road

🏆 Tadipatri's Best Kept Flavour Secret

From the very first day, Classic Rider Cafe has been a passion project — a place where bikers, families and food lovers gather over signature shawarmas, smoky grilled chicken and fiery peri peri specials, all crafted personally under the supervision of Mr. Maruthi Reddy.

We obsess over top-notch hygiene, fresh ingredients and quick, friendly service — whether you dine in, drive through, or order contactless delivery.
